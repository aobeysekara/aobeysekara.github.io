## Now

Tech Lead and Developer in industry, building large-scale simulation and AI
systems. Day to day that means shaping technical direction, mentoring engineers,
and writing code across the simulation, distributed-systems and ML stack.

## Background

PhD in Computational Physics, Imperial College London (2018) — numerical
modelling of hydraulic fracturing in naturally fractured rock, supervised under
the AMCG group. MPhys (hons) Physics, University of Southampton.

Before moving full-time into industry I spent several years developing and
applying CFD and HPC methods to multiphase flow, fluid-structure interaction
and fracture problems. That research record is still public — see
[Publications](/publications) and the [Google Scholar
profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=p4oWWqQAAAAJ&hl=en).

## What I work on

- Distributed simulation and high-performance computing
- Numerical methods, computational physics and model validation
- Machine learning — generative models, classification, ML-augmented simulation
- Engineering leadership: technical direction, team-building, code quality
